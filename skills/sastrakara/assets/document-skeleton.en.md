# Summary

Three to five sentences for a reader who has one minute. Say what this is,
what it is for, and the one thing they most need to know.

# Who This Is For

Name the readers (for example: leadership, the business team, partners) and
what they will be able to do after reading.

# The Big Picture

Describe the system the way you would to a new colleague. An everyday
comparison is welcome, for example: *"This page is like the notice board in
the office lobby. Anyone can read it, but nobody can change what is on it from
there."*

2. The system fetches data from …
3. The result is shown as …
